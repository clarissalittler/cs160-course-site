Unit 2 Overview

In this unit, you will learn how the design of the internet allows information to be shared across billions of people and devices. You will explore the problems the original designers of the internet had to solve.

During the unit, you will research social dilemmas driven by the ubiquity of the internet and the way it works.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Describe the problems the internet was designed to solve and how it solves these problems. 
  2. Explain how the design of the internet allowed it to grow or evolve.  
  3. Explain how different protocol layers are involved in an internet request. 
  4. Discuss how we might resolve dilemmas caused by the structure and continuing growth of the internet.  

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

    1. Complete Lessons 1 - 10 (LOs 1-4)
    2. Complete Quiz 2 (LOs 1-4)
    3. Complete and submit Week 2 Assignment (LOs 1-4)
    4. (Group) Post to discussion Unit 2 Lesson 6 - Battleship Protocol (LOs 1-4)
    5. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 2 Lesson 7 - Packet Protocol (LOs 1-4)
