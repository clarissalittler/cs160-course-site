## What is the internet?

There are a *lot* of pieces to the internet, and we're going to cover them all in this unit. But let's start with the big picture.

Take a look at Figure 2.1. It shows an overview of what you'll be learning about in this module. Here's the basic question we're trying to answer: when you type a web address into your browser, what actually happens? How does the information get from wherever it lives out there in the world to your screen?

![Internet overview diagram](../images/internet%20overview.png)
*Figure 2.1. Overview of the internet (Image Source: [Micro Chip Developer](https://microchip.wdfiles.com/local--files/tcpip:detailed-tcpip-communication/detailed_step_1.png))*

It's one of those things that feels almost magical when you stop and think about it. You click a link and --- boom --- a page appears. But there's a whole chain of events happening under the hood, and it's honestly pretty fascinating once you see how it all fits together.
