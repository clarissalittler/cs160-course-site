Unit 2 Overview

In this unit, we're going to figure out how the internet actually works — how its design lets billions of people and devices share information with each other. We'll dig into the problems the original designers had to solve and see how their solutions were clever enough to scale from a handful of university computers to, well, everything.

Along the way, you'll research some of the social dilemmas that come from having a worldwide network that nobody fully controls. Turns out, connecting everyone to everything has some tricky side effects.

Learning objectives

After this unit, you'll be able to:

  1. Describe the problems the internet was designed to solve — and how it actually solves them.
  2. Explain how the internet's design allowed it to grow and evolve far beyond what its creators imagined.
  3. Explain how different protocol layers work together when you make an internet request.
  4. Discuss how we might resolve dilemmas caused by the structure and continuing growth of the internet.
