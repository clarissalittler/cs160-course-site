# Future Lesson Topics

These are topics identified as gaps for a 2026 gen-ed CS course. Clarissa plans to adapt her own lecture materials for these.

## 1. Practical AI Literacy / Prompt Engineering

**Suggested home:** Unit 9, between Lesson 10 (LLMs) and Lesson 11 (Ethics)

Students in 2026 are using ChatGPT, Copilot, &c. daily. Unit 9 teaches *what* AI is and how it works, but not *how to use AI tools effectively*. Topics to cover:

- Writing clear, specific prompts (and why vague prompts get vague answers)
- Understanding why AI gives bad answers (connecting back to the hallucination discussion)
- Verifying AI output --- when to trust it, when to double-check
- Iterating on prompts (refining, adding constraints, asking for different formats)
- Ethical use of AI tools in academic and professional settings

## 2. Misinformation and Synthetic Media

**Suggested home:** Unit 9 or Unit 10 (connects to both)

The specific challenge of navigating a world full of AI-generated content. Topics to cover:

- Deepfakes --- AI-generated images, audio, and video
- How to evaluate whether content is real (reverse image search, metadata, source verification)
- The scale of the problem --- how easy it is to generate convincing fake content
- Filter bubbles and algorithmic amplification of misinformation
- Developing personal habits for information verification
- Media literacy as a civic skill

## 3. Environmental Impact of Computing

**Suggested home:** Unit 10 Lesson 10 (Computing Innovations) or standalone lesson

Students should know their digital activity has physical-world consequences. Topics to cover:

- Energy consumption of data centers (and the scale --- a single large data center can use as much power as a small city)
- The carbon cost of training large AI models
- E-waste --- what happens to old devices, rare earth minerals, global supply chains
- Streaming video as a surprisingly large share of internet energy use
- What individuals and companies can do (and the limits of individual action vs. systemic change)
