Data Analysis and Quantitative Reasoning

Quantitative Reasoning is the ability to use mathematics and information to solve real world problems. Used in combination with datasets, it will enable students to think critically about data. Students will learn computational, mathematical, and statistical methods for analyzing data, drawing inferences, and making predictions to answer questions.
