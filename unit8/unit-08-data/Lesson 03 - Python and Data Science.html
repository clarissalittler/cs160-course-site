What is data science?

Data science, in its most basic terms, can be defined as obtaining insights and information — really, anything of value — out of data. In practice, data science usually works with digital data because computers need machine-readable data to analyze it efficiently. Data can start in the physical world as sound, images, sensor readings, or survey responses, but it is typically collected or converted into digital form for analysis.

Data science overlaps with computer science, mathematics, and statistics. Computer science is broader than data science; data science focuses on extracting insight from data using programming, statistics, and domain knowledge.
