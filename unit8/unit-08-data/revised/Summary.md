# Unit 8 Summary

## Wrap Up

In this unit, we learned how data analysis turns raw data into useful information about the world. We used data visualization libraries in Python to find patterns hidden inside datasets. We also investigated how misleading charts get used in the media to shift opinions --- and why quantitative reasoning is so important for spotting misinformation.

> **Key Takeaways**
>
> - We can develop real insights and knowledge about our world by manipulating and visualizing data --- in particular, by finding patterns.
> - When we investigate two columns of data, we can observe patterns that reveal relationships between them (correlation).
> - But here's the important part --- we can't know *for certain* what's causing the correlation. Correlation isn't causation, &c.
