## What is data science?

Data science, in its most basic terms, is about obtaining insights and information --- really, anything of value --- out of data. In the context of data science, the only form of data that matters is **digital data**: information represented in a digital format that can be stored, processed, and analyzed by computers.

Here's a useful way to think about how data science relates to computer science: computer science deals with programming software and hardware, while data science deals with analytics, programming, *and* statistics. There's overlap, but data science requires a lot more knowledge of math and statistics than typical software development does.

That's actually one of the things that makes data science such an interesting field --- it sits right at the intersection of programming, mathematics, and domain expertise. You need all three to do it well.
