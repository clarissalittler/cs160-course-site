Lesson 1 — Data and Dataset

After this lesson, you'll be able to:

  • Explain what data and datasets are.
  • Give examples of different types of datasets.

What is data?

Data are observations or measurements — whether raw or processed — represented as text, numbers, or multimedia. That's a pretty broad definition, and it should be! Data is everywhere. It's one of the key ingredients that makes AI work.

What is a dataset?

A dataset is a structured collection of data, generally associated with a unique body of work. Think of it as a neatly organized package of related information — a spreadsheet of customer orders, a folder of medical images, a collection of audio recordings.

How to Create a Dataset for Machine Learning | #AI101

Dataset types

Here's where it gets interesting — datasets aren't just spreadsheets full of numbers. They come in all kinds of formats:

Text datasets

  • Ticket datasets (like customer support tickets)
  • EHR data & physician dictation transcripts
  • Document datasets

Speech datasets

  • Monologue speech
  • Dialogue speech
  • Acoustic data
  • Natural language utterances

Image datasets

  • Document datasets
  • Facial datasets
  • Healthcare data
  • Hand gesture data

Video datasets

  • Human posture video datasets
  • CCTV/surveillance video datasets
  • Traffic video datasets

That variety is worth pausing on. When we say "data," most people picture rows and columns in a spreadsheet. But an AI might be trained on thousands of hours of video footage or millions of photographs. It's all data.
