## Data Analysis and Quantitative Reasoning

Quantitative reasoning is *the ability to use mathematics and information to solve real-world problems*. When you combine it with datasets, you can start thinking critically about data --- not just reading numbers, but actually understanding what they mean. We'll be learning computational, mathematical, and statistical methods for analyzing data, drawing inferences, and making predictions to answer questions.

That's a fancy way of saying: we're going to look at data and figure stuff out.
