Data Analysis and Quantitative Reasoning

Quantitative reasoning is the ability to use mathematics and information to solve real world problems - which, pointed at a dataset, is exactly what you've been practicing all unit. This lesson pulls it together with full worked examples: asking questions of a dataset, drawing the right graphs, and interpreting what comes back - the same computational, mathematical, and statistical moves you'll use to draw inferences and make predictions of your own.
