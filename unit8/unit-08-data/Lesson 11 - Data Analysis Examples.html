Data Analysis and Quantitative Reasoning

Quantitative reasoning is the ability to use mathematics and information to solve real-world problems. Applied to a dataset, that is exactly what we've been practicing throughout the unit. This lesson brings the pieces together with worked examples: asking questions, choosing graphs, and interpreting the results.
