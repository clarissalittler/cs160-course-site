Week 10: Make a Mini-Zine

Open this file in an editor. Replace the bracketed prompts with your own work.
Keep eight numbered pages/sections. You may submit this linear text version;
it uses the same rubric as a folded zine. Images and folding are optional.
Choose any computer science topic that interests you and an audience you want
to reach. Your topic may come from any course unit or go beyond the course.
The prompts below are optional: adapt them for an explanation, guide, essay,
story, poetry, comic, or another form that communicates your computer science
topic. Use only personal details you want to share.
