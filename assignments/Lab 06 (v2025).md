# Lab 06 - Mini-Zine

It's time to make an 8 page mini-'zine about a topic you find interesting in computer science. What's an 8 page mini-'zine? Glad you asked! It's a small document you can make out of a single piece of paper, dividing it into 8 pages that, with a single cut and some folding, makes a tiny booklet! Look at the following to get more of an idea of how they're made.

* [https://tahoetrailguide.com/making-an-8-page-zine-from-a-single-sheet-of-paper/](https://tahoetrailguide.com/making-an-8-page-zine-from-a-single-sheet-of-paper/)
* [https://goofygeekentertainment.itch.io/how-to-make-a-pint-size-zine](https://goofygeekentertainment.itch.io/how-to-make-a-pint-size-zine)

This is *not* meant to be a difficult task. Just an opportunity to learn a bit more about a topic and share with your fellow students something you think is neat. You can make it a wall of text, a comic, a piece of collage art! Whatever works for how you want to convey the information!

There are a few ways you could make this mini-'zine:

* You can make it completely by hand, drawing out the pages with the right layout like in the links above, then scan it, print it, cut it, fold it
* The *easiest* option: download the Electric Zine Maker from itchio ([https://alienmelon.itch.io/electric-zine-maker](https://alienmelon.itch.io/electric-zine-maker)) and use the default layout and make your pages however you want then export to pdf when you're ready

What kinds of topics would work for a mini-zine? Basically anything you could try to summarize in just a couple of minutes. Which, for us, means something like:

* Pick a programming language, other than Python, and explain what people use it for and what makes it interesting
* Explain the rough idea of a very specific topic (transformers in machine learning, qubits, amortization, the A* path finding algorithm, how chess AI works, the gopher protocol)
* A rant! Do you think diffusion models are the coolest thing ever? An evil invention that will lead to a dystopia? Rant away on this or any other (CS related) topic and make your case in limited space.
