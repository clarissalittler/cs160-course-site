Final Project: Make a Mini-Zine

Open this file in any text editor or word processor and replace the bracketed
prompts with your own work. Keep the eight numbered pages/sections. You can
submit this linear text version as is: it's graded with the same rubric as a
folded zine, and images and folding are optional.

Pick any computer science topic that interests you (from any unit of the
course, or beyond it) and someone you'd like to explain it to. The prompts
below are only suggestions, so bend them to fit whatever you're making,
whether that's a how-to guide, a story, or a comic. Only include personal
details you're comfortable sharing.
