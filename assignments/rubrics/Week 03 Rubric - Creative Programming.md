# Week 3 Rubric — Creative Programming

This rubric applies equally to the turtle drawing, mini-zine, and pattern-poem routes. Score each row independently. Half-points are welcome. Add the five scores (20 points possible), then divide by 5 for the 0–4 assignment score.

| Criterion | 4 — Complete and effective | 3 — Mostly demonstrated | 2 — Partly demonstrated | 1 — Beginning evidence | 0 — No evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Execution and result | Runs top to bottom and produces a complete, intentional artifact. | Runs with a minor interruption or produces a nearly complete artifact. | Substantial sections run or the intended result is partly produced despite errors. | Some submitted code runs or clearly begins the artifact. | Starter is untouched or no relevant work. |
| Instructions and variables | Uses at least six meaningful tutorial-appropriate instructions and uses values/variables intentionally. | Uses six instructions with one weak/redundant use, or has five strong uses. | Uses three or four meaningful instructions or shows partial control of values. | Uses one or two relevant instructions. | No meaningful instructions. |
| Repetition with a loop | A for-loop correctly creates an intentional repeated part of the artifact. | Loop works but its contribution is slight or has a minor issue. | A recognizable loop is present but errors or placement limit the result. | Attempts a loop with relevant body code. | No loop evidence. |
| Original intention and development | Final work meaningfully transforms the starter/tutorial and communicates a coherent idea. | Work is recognizably new but modest or retains some unnecessary starter material. | Some original choices are visible alongside substantial copied/unfinished material. | States or begins an original intention. | Complete example is copied unchanged or final cell is untouched. |
| Clarity, accessibility, and reflection | Uses readable names/labels, supplies an accessible representation or sample run, and completes the reflection. | All are present with one minor clarity or completeness gap. | Two of the three areas provide useful evidence. | One area provides useful evidence. | None are provided. |

Partial-credit notes: runtime errors do not erase credit for a working loop, meaningful instructions, design choices, or reflection. Grade the route selected, not the student's choice of medium or visual polish.
