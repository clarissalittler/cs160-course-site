# Week 10 Rubric — Staying-Safe-Online Mini-Zine

Score each row independently. Half-points are welcome. Add the five scores (20 points possible), then divide by 5 for the 0–4 assignment score.

| Criterion | 4 — Complete and effective | 3 — Mostly demonstrated | 2 — Partly demonstrated | 1 — Beginning evidence | 0 — No evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Format and completion | All 8 pages, including covers, are present in a usable mini-zine sequence. | All pages are present with one sparse or unclear page. | Four to six developed pages or a substantially complete sequence. | Cover, outline, or one to three developed pages. | No zine evidence. |
| Unit 10 conceptual coverage | Clearly and correctly integrates at least three distinct Unit 10 concepts around one focused topic. | Three concepts are present but one is lightly developed, or two are developed especially well. | Two concepts are meaningfully covered or three are only briefly named. | One relevant Unit 10 concept is introduced. | No relevant Unit 10 content. |
| Accuracy and practical safety advice | Advice is accurate, appropriately qualified, actionable, and avoids security myths or unrealistic guarantees. | Advice is generally accurate with one minor omission or overstatement. | A mix of useful advice and a significant gap/inaccuracy that can be revised. | Some relevant advice is present but is vague or unreliable. | No usable safety advice. |
| Audience and organization | Identifies a real audience, anticipates its needs, and organizes information so readers can understand and act on it. | Audience and sequence are clear with one thin or confusing section. | Audience or organization is apparent but inconsistently supported. | Names or implies an audience and begins a relevant structure. | No audience-focused evidence. |
| Accessible and intentional communication | Text and visuals are legible, key meaning does not depend only on color or decoration, sources/attributions are included when needed, and presentation supports the message. | Communication is usable with one minor accessibility, readability, or attribution gap. | Meaning is mostly recoverable but several design/readability issues interfere. | Some content is readable or an accessible alternative is provided. | Submission cannot be interpreted. |

Partial-credit notes: do not grade artistic skill, access to design software, printing quality, or whether the zine is handmade versus digital. Credit content page by page and concept by concept; one inaccurate claim should affect only the evidence it touches.
