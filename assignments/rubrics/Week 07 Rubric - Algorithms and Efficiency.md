# Week 7 Rubric — Algorithms and Efficiency

Score each row independently. Half-points are welcome. Add the five scores (20 points possible), then divide by 5 for the 0–4 assignment score.

| Criterion | 4 — Complete and accurate | 3 — Mostly demonstrated | 2 — Partly demonstrated | 1 — Beginning evidence | 0 — No evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Good-algorithm requirements (Q1) | Correctly identifies and explains all three violated requirements and rewrites step 5 with a definite stopping condition. | Three or four subparts are correct with one minor terminology/reasoning gap. | Two subparts are correct or all four show partial understanding. | One subpart contains a relevant idea. | No relevant work. |
| Language-independent algorithm (Q2) | Correct result/meaning, all four Python–JavaScript skeleton matches, and a clear explanation of language-independent sameness. | Most parts are correct with one omission or minor mismatch. | The computed meaning or several structural matches are correct, with a partial explanation. | Identifies one relevant behavior or cross-language match. | No relevant work. |
| Binary-search trace (Q3) | Shows each middle value, direction, remaining portion, and correct comparison count through finding 72. | Search path is essentially correct with one trace/count omission or arithmetic slip. | Several correct search steps show the halving process, but the trace is incomplete. | Identifies a relevant middle/direction step. | No relevant trace. |
| Big-O classification (Q4) | All four classifications and explanations correctly connect work growth to input size. | Three classifications/reasons are correct or all four have only one minor reasoning gap. | Two classifications are correct or all four contain relevant partial reasoning. | One relevant classification or growth explanation is present. | No relevant work. |
| Reasoning and communication | Answers consistently show why, not only final labels; work is complete and easy to follow. | Reasoning is present for most answers with one thin area. | Some useful reasoning is visible, though several conclusions are unsupported. | At least one answer includes relevant reasoning. | No reasoning beyond unrelated material. |

Partial-credit notes: score every lettered subpart. Correct reasoning with a mislabeled term deserves substantial credit; a correct label with no requested explanation earns credit for the classification but not its missing support.
