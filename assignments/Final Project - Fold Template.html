Final Project — Numbered Fold Template

Choose any computer science topic that interests you, from any course unit or beyond the course.

This is a blank sheet for printing and writing by hand, or a page-order guide for a digital layout. For an editable linear version, download the eight-page text template. Both formats use the same assignment rubric.

Print: US Letter, landscape, one-sided, with browser headers and footers off. Check that the print preview shows one sheet. Then trim off the outside margin along the printed border, and what's left divides into eight equal panels. The printed numbers are just guides, so you can write over them or trace the layout onto another sheet.

Order: top row 5, 4, 3, 2 upside down; bottom row 6, 7, 8, 1 upright. (On a narrow screen, like a phone, this page shows the panels in reading order instead.)

  1. Crease the four columns and the horizontal middle line, then unfold.
  2. Cut only the dashed horizontal segment across the middle two panels. Keep the outer panels joined.
  3. Fold along the horizontal middle line with the content facing out. Push the short ends toward each other so the slit opens into a cross.
  4. Fold the panels into a booklet with page 1 at the front and page 8 at the back. Check the reading order.

The Final Project handout, in the Assignments area in D2L, has the requirements and submission directions. If you'd rather skip the printer and the folding, the linear version works just as well.
