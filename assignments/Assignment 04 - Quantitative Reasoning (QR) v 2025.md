## Quantitative Reasoning (Data)

## Purpose

In this assignment you will be provided a dataset, and asked to obtain insights and information, really anything of value out of data. You will also draw insightful conclusions and consider the societal impact of the information provided by your charts.

In order to do this, we start with a question that you can answer with the dataset, identify the columns of information needed to answer the question, represent the data using graphs or charts, and interpret the charts and come to conclusions.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

* Draw conclusions from data by using quantitative reasoning, including reading charts, graphs, or tables.
* Explain the societal impact of the Data Analysis using insights and information obtained from the dataset.
