Unit 9 Overview

In this unit, you'll explore Artificial Intelligence — what it actually is, how it works under the hood, and why it matters for your life right now. AI isn't just a buzzword or a far-off sci-fi concept; it's already shaping how you search the web, get medical diagnoses, apply for jobs, and interact with your phone every day. Understanding AI — even at a high level — is a crucial part of being an informed digital citizen. You'll come away from this unit with the vocabulary and conceptual grounding to think critically about AI tools, their limitations, and their societal impacts.

Learning objectives

At the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  1. Explain key terminology associated with AI, including machine learning, neural networks, and large language models.
  2. Explain, at a high level, how machine learning and deep learning work.
  3. Describe how large language models generate text and identify their limitations.
  4. Discuss the societal impacts of AI, including bias, ethics, and everyday applications.
  5. Use an LLM effectively and evaluate its output critically.

Learning activities

You are expected to complete the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1 - 11 (LOs 1-5)
  2. Complete Quiz 9 (LOs 1-5)
  3. Complete and submit Lab 5 (LOs 1-5)
  4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 9 AI Bias (LO 4)
  5. (Partner) Post to discussion Unit 9 AI in Everyday Life (LO 4)
