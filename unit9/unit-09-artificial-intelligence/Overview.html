Unit 9 Overview

In this unit, you will explore a branch of computer science called Artificial Intelligence and its impact on our daily lives. An understanding of AI will be a crucial part of being an informed digital citizen. AI has and will have a societal impact, and it has the potential to be a powerful tool for social good.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Explain key terminology associated with the field of AI. 
  2. Explain, at a high level, how Machine Learning works. 
  3. Define the different Machine Learning algorithms and their applications.
  4. Differentiate between Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
  5. Discuss the different societal impacts of AI and the ethical considerations while working with AI.

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks achieve the above objectives:

    1. Complete Lessons 1 - 12 (LOs 1-5)
    2. Complete Quiz 9 (LOs 1-5)
    3. Complete and submit Lab 5 (LOs 1-5)
    4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 9 Lesson 2 - Replika Chatbot (LO 3)
    5. (Partner) Post to discussion Unit 9 Lesson 5 - Virtual Assistants (LO 3)
