Unit 9 Overview

This unit is about Artificial Intelligence - the technology behind ChatGPT, image generators, and a hundred quieter things in your pocket. But instead of treating AI as magic, we're going to open the hood. The unit comes in two halves. In the first half you'll learn, genuinely, how a machine learns - a single idea (adjusting numbers to shrink a mistake) that you'll watch happen in hands-on playgrounds, from fitting a line all the way up to neural networks and the famous one-two punch of gradient descent and backpropagation. In the second half, armed with that understanding, you'll explore the world these methods built: the decade-long deep learning revolution, the recent explosion of giant models, how to use generative AI well, and the real questions of bias, ethics, and who decides. The goal is for you to walk out seeing AI not as magic or menace, but as math, data, and human choices - which is exactly what an informed citizen needs.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Explain what AI is today, and the difference between narrow AI and general AI (AGI).
  2. Explain how machine learning learns from examples instead of hand-written rules.
  3. Describe, in plain terms, how a model learns - gradient descent and backpropagation tuning a network's "knobs."
  4. Describe what neural networks and deep learning are, and how the deep learning revolution and the recent boom in large models came about.
  5. Use generative AI tools thoughtfully, and discuss the societal impacts, bias, and ethics of AI.

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1 - 11 (LOs 1-5)
  2. Complete Quiz 9 (LOs 1-5)
