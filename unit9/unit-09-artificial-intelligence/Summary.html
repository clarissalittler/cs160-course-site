Unit 9 Summary

Wrap up

In this unit, you explored a branch of computer science called Artificial Intelligence and its impact on our daily lives. An understanding of AI will be a crucial part of being an informed digital citizen. AI has and will have a societal impact, and it has the potential to be a powerful tool for social good. 

Summary

  • There are several subsets of AI including Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning.
  • Machine learning uses different types of training: supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement.
  • Linear Regression uses linear relationships between a dependent variable (target) and one or more independent variables (predictors) to predict the future of the target.
  • There are many different career opportunities in AI including AI researcher, AI/ML engineer, data scientist, data policymaker, AI ethicist, AI artist, and NLP developer, for example.
