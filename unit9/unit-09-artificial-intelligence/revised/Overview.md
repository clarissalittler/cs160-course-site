# Unit 9 Overview

Welcome to Unit 9! This is the one you've probably been waiting for --- we're talking about **artificial intelligence**. Not the science fiction version with sentient robots and rogue supercomputers, but the real thing: the AI that's already woven into your daily life and reshaping the world around you.

We'll start by defining what AI actually is (and isn't), then work through how machine learning models learn from data --- including supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. You'll get hands-on with linear regression to predict housing prices, explore how neural networks and large language models work under the hood, and dig into the ethical questions that make AI one of the most important topics in technology today. By the end, you'll understand AI well enough to have *informed* opinions about it --- and that matters more than you might think.

## Learning objectives

**After this unit, you'll be able to:**

1. Define artificial intelligence and distinguish how real-world AI differs from science fiction depictions.
2. Explain how machine learning models learn from data, including the differences between supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.
3. Apply linear regression to make predictions from data and evaluate how well a model fits.
4. Describe how neural networks, deep learning, and large language models work at a conceptual level.
5. Analyze ethical issues in AI --- including bias, fairness, and societal impact --- and explain why they matter.
