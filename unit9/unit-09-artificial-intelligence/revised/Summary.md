# Unit 9 Summary

## Wrap Up

In this unit, we explored what artificial intelligence really is --- not science fiction, but software that finds patterns in data and uses them to make predictions. We learned how machine learning models are trained, got hands-on with linear regression, looked under the hood of neural networks and large language models, and wrestled with the ethical questions that come with deploying these systems in the real world.

> **Key Takeaways**
>
> - AI is pattern-finding in data. Machine learning comes in three flavors --- supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement --- each suited to different kinds of problems.
> - Linear regression is a foundational technique that gives you intuition for how all models work: fit a line, make predictions, evaluate the fit.
> - LLMs are powerful next-token predictors, but they're prone to *hallucination* --- confidently generating things that aren't true. Understanding this is essential for using them responsibly.
> - AI systems can inherit and amplify bias from their training data, and as informed citizens we all have a role in shaping how these technologies are built and regulated.
