Unit 11 Overview - Finals Week

You made it to the last week! There are no new lessons this week. There's the final exam, a couple of short surveys, and one last page of closing thoughts, which I'd love for you to read before you go.

What to do this week

  1. Complete the Post-Survey and the Course Design Survey.
  2. Take the Final Exam. You'll see it in the content area once you've completed the Post-Survey and have a score of at least a 1 on each of the Week 1–9 Assignments. (The final project isn't required to unlock it.)
  3. Read Closing Words: Where Computing Goes from Here.

Check the Course Schedule for this term's due dates. Resubmissions for the weekly assignments closed on the Friday of Week 10.

Studying for the final

A good way to review is to go back through each unit's Summary page. Each one ends with the ideas worth carrying forward, and if you can explain each of those in your own words, with an example, you're in good shape. The unit quizzes and the Python Practice Problems are also good practice, and so is rereading your own weekly assignments along with the feedback you got on them.
