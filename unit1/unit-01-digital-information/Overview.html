Unit 1 Overview

In this unit, you will explore the way computers store and represent complex information like numbers, text, images, and sound.

We will begin by investigating what it means to represent information, with a challenge to build your own representation systems. You will learn the ideas behind real-world systems used to represent complex information and the challenges that arise from digitizing information, such as the need to compress it or intellectual property questions.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Describe how computers represent information. 
  2. Represent numbers using the binary number system. 
  3. Explain how bits can be arranged to represent text and digital images. 
  4. Determine if lossless or lossy compression should be used for a given situation. 
  5. Explain how copyright and Creative Commons Licenses can be applied to digital works of creativity. 

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

    1. Complete Lessons 1 - 10 (LOs 1-5)
    2. Complete Quiz 1 
    3. Complete and submit Lab 1 (LOs 1, 2, 3, 5) 
    4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 1 Lesson 2 - Circle Square Patterns (LO 2)
    5. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 1 Lesson 6 - Black and White Digital Images (LO 3)
    6. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 1 Lesson 10 - Intellectual Property (LO 5)

    If you have questions, you can post them in the Course Lobby or send your instructor an email.
