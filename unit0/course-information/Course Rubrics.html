Rubrics

Weekly Assignments are graded on a 4.0 scale with a rubric. Rubrics clarify the content and outcome of your learning and show you where you are at and what you need to do to improve. 

Weekly Assignment rubrics

You have ten Weekly Assignments in this course, one per unit. Every assignment is graded on the same 0-4 scale:

  • 4 (Exceeds): the work runs or reads cleanly, meets every requirement, and communicates a clear intention.
  • 3 (Meets): the work meets most requirements, with one element missing or a minor issue.
  • 2 (Progressing): real work is present, but a requirement is unmet or errors keep it from coming together.
  • 1 (Beginning): an attempt was made, but the work does not yet run or hold together as a complete artifact.
  • 0 (No Evidence): nothing was submitted.

Each Week N Assignment handout spells out exactly what these levels mean for that week's work — read the criteria there before you submit. After feedback, you can revise and resubmit for full credit until the Friday before Final Exam week. You must score at least a 1 on every Weekly Assignment to unlock the Final Exam.

The Week 10 Assignment is the course's final project (a digital-safety mini-zine) and is due at the end of the term. Submit early if you want a chance to revise after feedback — resubmissions close the Friday before Final Exam week, so work handed in right at the deadline cannot be revised.
