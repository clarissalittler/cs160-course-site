Grading Rubrics

Rubrics

Labs and Assignments will be graded on a 4.0 scale with a Rubric. Rubrics clarify the content and outcome of your learning and show you where you are at and what you need to do to improve. 

In the rubrics below, a score of 0 indicates No Evidence - no submission was made.

Lab rubrics

You have six labs to complete in this course. The first five labs assess your knowledge of the content in the unit lessons. You can submit your labs and look at where you are on the rubric and submit any changes for improvement 1 time. Once your lab is assigned a grade, the solution key will open and you can assess your own work.

Week 10 Assignment is a presentation on a computing innovation and is due at the end of the term. If you submit early, you have the opportunity to submit changes for improvement 1 time. Week 10 Assignment presentations submitted on the due date will not be allowed corrections.

Assignment rubrics

You have four programming assignments in this course. Programming assignments must be submitted by the due date and can be resubmitted 2 times for corrections. Carefully read all of the feedback and review your score on the rubric before resubmitting. No resubmissions will be allowed during the last week of the term. You must score at least a 1 on all assignments to unlock the final exam during final exam week.
