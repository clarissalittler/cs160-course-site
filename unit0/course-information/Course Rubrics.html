Grading Rubrics

Rubrics

Weekly Assignments are graded on a 4.0 scale with an analytic rubric. Rubrics clarify the content and outcome of your learning, show what you have already demonstrated, and identify the next change that will improve your work.

Weekly Assignment rubrics

You have ten Weekly Assignments in this course, one per unit. Each rubric has five independently scored criteria worth 0–4 points each, for 20 points total. Your rubric total is divided by 5 to produce the assignment score on the course's 0–4 scale. For example, 17.5 out of 20 rubric points is a 3.5 out of 4.0.

  • Partial credit is built in: each criterion is scored separately, and half-points may be awarded when work falls between two descriptions.
  • Working evidence still counts: a wrong final answer or a program error does not erase credit for a correct setup, useful trace, working function, test, documentation, design choice, or reflection.
  • There is no single-error score cap: one missing requirement affects only the relevant criterion. Scores from all five criteria are added together.
  • 0 means no evidence: a criterion receives 0 only when the submission contains no relevant evidence for it. A completely absent submission receives 0 for the assignment.

Read the rubric for your week before you submit. After feedback, you can revise and resubmit for full credit until the Friday before Final Exam week. You must score at least a 1 on every Weekly Assignment to unlock the Final Exam.

The Week 10 Assignment is the course's final project (a digital-safety mini-zine) and is due at the end of the term. Submit early if you want a chance to revise after feedback — resubmissions close the Friday before Final Exam week, so work handed in right at the deadline cannot be revised.
