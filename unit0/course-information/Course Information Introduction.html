Course Information Introduction

This module will give you information about your course.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Identify your instructor
  2. List the main course requirements from the syllabus
  3. Identify the things you will get out of this course (course outcomes)
  4. Review the course schedule and identify what your weekly obligations are
  5. Explain how your work will be evaluated

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks in order to achieve the above objectives:

    1. Read the introduction from your instructor
    2. Read the course syllabus, course schedule, grading rubrics, communicating online, PCC policies, privacy policies, and land acknowledgment pages
    3. Post to the Introduce Yourself! discussion topic
    4. Post to the Find a Study Group! discussion topic
    5. Complete the Course Pre-Survey
    6. Complete the Course Information Quiz with a 100% to unlock the course content

    If you have questions, you can post them in the Course Lobby or send your instructor an email.

    Good luck!
