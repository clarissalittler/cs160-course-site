# Unit 7 Overview

![Unit 7 banner](img/banner.jpg)

This unit is where things get really fun --- we're diving into **algorithms**. That word might sound intimidating, but algorithms are just step-by-step instructions for solving problems. You already use them every day; you just don't call them that.

What we're really doing here is learning to *think* like a computer scientist. That means recognizing patterns, figuring out the best approach to a problem, and --- crucially --- caring about whether our approach is any good. Not all solutions are created equal, and we'll see exactly why.

## After this unit, you'll be able to:

1. Write simple algorithms and describe what makes an algorithm *actually* good.
2. Understand and analyze basic searching and sorting algorithms.
3. Describe and evaluate algorithm efficiency --- how quickly can the computer crunch through your solution?
4. Quantify efficiency using Big-O classification (it's less scary than it sounds, promise).
