Unit 7 Overview

In this unit, you'll learn what algorithms are, how to compare them, and why some are dramatically faster than others. You'll explore searching and sorting — two of the most fundamental problems in computer science — and learn how computer scientists measure efficiency using something called Big-O notation.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Define what an algorithm is and describe the characteristics of a good one.
  2. Explain and trace through linear search, binary search, selection sort, and insertion sort.
  3. Analyze algorithm efficiency by counting operations and understanding why this matters.
  4. Classify algorithms using Big-O notation (O(1), O(n), O(log n), O(n²)).

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1–9 (LOs 1–4)
  2. Complete Quiz 7 (LOs 1–4)
  3. Complete and submit Lab 4 (LOs 1–4)
  4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 7 Lesson 8 — Sorting Efficiencies (LOs 1, 2, 3)

If you have questions, you can post them in the Course Lobby or send your instructor an email.
