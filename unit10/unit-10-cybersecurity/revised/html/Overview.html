Unit 10 Overview

Welcome to Unit 10! This is the final unit of the course, and we're going out with something that affects every single one of you every single day — cybersecurity and privacy. We'll dig into how your personal data gets collected and sold, how attackers exploit human psychology and technical vulnerabilities, and what you can actually do to protect yourself.

But this unit isn't just about defense. We'll also explore how encryption works — from ancient ciphers to the public key cryptography that secures every online transaction you make — and we'll look at real-world data breaches to understand what went wrong and why. Finally, we'll zoom out and think about computing innovations more broadly: the ways technology simultaneously makes our lives better and creates new risks. It's the perfect lens for wrapping up everything we've learned this term.

Learning objectives

After this unit, you'll be able to:

  1. Explain data privacy policies and how personal data is collected, used, and sold.
  2. Identify social engineering attacks and technical threats — phishing, malware, &c. — and explain how they work.
  3. Describe how passwords, multi-factor authentication, and encryption protect data.
  4. Analyze real-world data breaches and explain what went wrong.
  5. Evaluate the dual impacts of computing innovations on society.
