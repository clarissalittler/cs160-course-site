Unit 10 Summary

Wrap up

In this unit, you learned how computing innovations have impacted our world in beneficial and harmful ways. You learned how data can pose a threat to our privacy and security and the ways that encryption and other techniques are used to protect it. 

Summary

  • The cost for accessing some sites is our data.
  • Read a website's privacy policy before giving your data.
  • Keylogging, phishing, and malware are security risks.
  • Keylogging is the use of a program to record every keystroke made by a computer user in order to gain fraudulent access to passwords and other confidential information.
  • Phishing is a technique that attempts to trick a user into providing personal information.
  • Malware is software intended to damage a computing system or to take partial control over its operation. 
  • Data encryption is a strategy for protecting data.
  • Personal strategies for securing data include strong passwords, using multi-factor authentication, and updating your software.
