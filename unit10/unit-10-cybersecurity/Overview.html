Unit 10 Overview

In this unit, you will learn how data can pose a threat to our privacy and security and the ways that encryption and other techniques are used to protect it. You will learn how computing innovations have impacted our world in beneficial and harmful ways and do research and complete a presentation on a computing innovation.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Describe data that is used and collected by computing innovations. 
  2. Explain risks to privacy from using modern technology. 
  3. Explain security risks and their impact on society. 
  4. Explain encryption.
  5. Explain the benefits of multi-factor authentication. 

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

    1. Complete Lessons 1 - 5 (LOs 1-5)
    2. Complete Quiz 10 (LOs 1-5)
    3. Complete and submit Lab 6 (LOs 1-3)
    4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 10 Lesson 1 - Privacy Policies (LO 2)
    5. (Individual) Post to discussion Lab 6 - Computing Innovation Topic and Presentation (LOs 1-3)
