Unit 10 Overview

In this unit, you'll learn how your data gets collected, shared, and sometimes stolen — and what you can do about it. We'll cover everything from privacy policies to ransomware to AI-powered scams, and you'll come away with practical tools for protecting yourself online.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Explain what personally identifiable information is and how it's collected by apps and websites.
  2. Describe the tradeoffs between privacy, convenience, and security in modern technology.
  3. Identify common cyberattacks (phishing, social engineering, malware, ransomware) and how they work.
  4. Explain how encryption protects data and distinguish between symmetric and asymmetric encryption.
  5. Use practical strategies to protect your data: password managers, multi-factor authentication, and software updates.
  6. Describe how AI is being used to create more sophisticated cybersecurity threats.

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1–7 (LOs 1–6)
  2. Complete Quiz 10 (LOs 1–6)
  3. Complete and submit Lab 6 (LOs 1–3)
  4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 10 Lesson 1 — Privacy Policies (LO 1)

If you have questions, you can post them in the Course Lobby or send your instructor an email.
