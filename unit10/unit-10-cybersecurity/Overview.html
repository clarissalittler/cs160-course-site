Unit 10 Overview

This unit is a practical guide to protecting ourselves online. Many common threats do not resemble the dramatic hacking shown in movies. They include scams designed to manipulate people, false information designed or amplified to attract attention, and attempts to compromise accounts or collect personal data.

Technical safeguards matter, but a small set of habits can reduce risk substantially. We will practice recognizing phishing, checking online claims before sharing them, protecting accounts with appropriate authentication, managing privacy, responding to harassment, and recovering when a security problem occurs.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Recognize scams and social-engineering tactics, and spot phishing in its common forms.
  2. Evaluate online information for misinformation using a simple, repeatable verification routine.
  3. Protect your accounts with strong unique passwords, a password manager, and two-factor authentication.
  4. Manage your privacy and personal data, and protect yourself from online harassment.
  5. Respond effectively when something goes wrong - malware, a hacked account, or a scam.

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1 - 9 (LOs 1-5)
  2. Complete Quiz 10 (LOs 1-5)
  3. Complete and submit Week 10 Assignment (LOs 1-5)
  4. Complete and share the Week 10 Activity Discussion: Investigate a Scam
