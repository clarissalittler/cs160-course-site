Unit 10 Overview

This unit is a practical guide to protecting yourself online. Forget the Hollywood image of hooded hackers - the threats that actually reach everyday people are scams designed to trick you, a flood of misinformation engineered to mislead you, and attacks on your accounts and privacy. The good news, and the theme of the whole unit, is that staying safe is far less about technical wizardry than about a handful of smart habits. You'll learn to spot scams and phishing cold, to check whether something online is actually true before you believe or share it, to lock down your passwords and accounts, to manage your privacy and protect yourself from harassment, and to know exactly what to do if something goes wrong anyway.

Learning objectives

At the end of this learning module, you will be able to:

  1. Recognize scams and social-engineering tactics, and spot phishing in its common forms.
  2. Evaluate online information for misinformation using a simple, repeatable verification routine.
  3. Protect your accounts with strong unique passwords, a password manager, and two-factor authentication.
  4. Manage your privacy and personal data, and protect yourself from online harassment.
  5. Respond effectively when something goes wrong - malware, a hacked account, or a scam.

Learning activities

You are expected to do the following tasks to achieve the above objectives:

  1. Complete Lessons 1 - 9 (LOs 1-5)
  2. Complete Quiz 10 (LOs 1-5)
  3. Complete and submit Week 10 Assignment (LOs 1-5)
  4. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 10 - Scams in the Wild (LO 1)
  5. (Individual) Post to discussion Unit 10 - Privacy: Where Do You Draw the Line? (LO 4)
